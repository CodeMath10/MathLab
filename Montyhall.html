Should you Switch?

Welcome to the MathShow! Today, you're going to play a game with high stakes. You could walk away a millionaire—or the same as you are now. Good luck!

How to play?

The rules are simple:

1. First, choose any door.
2. One of the other doors will reveal a goat, and you will be given the choice to switch.
3. Once you have chosen the final door, you will get whatever is behind it.

Choose any door




So... Should you switch?

Unless you're deliberately trying to get a goat and not a car, switching is the more sensible choice. Most (⅔) of the time, you will initially pick a goat, meaning there is a ⅔ chance the car is behind one of the other two doors. Monty (the host) then reveals a goat behind one of them. Since he deliberately reveals a goat, that ⅔ probability is now concentrated on the one remaining unopened door. So, by switching, you have a ⅔ chance of winning!
